Method overview: from cameras to a safe robot move

Five stages, two phases. Perception finds who & where; prediction and safety decide where-next & act.

1Multi-camera capture
6 overhead (nadir) cameras. Straight-down view ⇒ image ≈ floor plane, so pixel → floor coordinate is nearly free.
2Detection & dataset
YOLO11 person, recall-first (conf 0.15). Clip-level train/val/test split; sim + real domains kept separate.
3World fusion & tracking
Affine calibration → shared floor (5 cm). Merge overlapping detections (same floor spot = same person) → Kalman + coast tracker gives stable IDs and velocity. Coverage recall 0.92–0.94.
4Trajectory prediction
CV / Kalman / LSTM · Transformer. Multimodal future paths + uncertainty σ, 1.6 s horizon. Learned models catch curved approaches.
5Risk & avoidance
ISO/TS 15066 SSM. If a predicted path enters the stop ring, the robot slows / stops / retreats before contact.

One line per stage. Overhead cameras (1) detect people (2); detections are fused onto one floor and tracked (3); each track's next 1.6 s is predicted (4); if a predicted path will enter the robot's stop ring, the robot acts before contact (5). Stages 1–3 are perception, 4–5 are prediction & control.
