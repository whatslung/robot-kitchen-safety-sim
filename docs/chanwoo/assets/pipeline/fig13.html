The arm pulls back over the kettle, off the worker's path (compare its reach in step 2). The worker passes through where the arm just was — no contact.

Where does it avoid to? Look at the arm across the three frames: extended out over the aisle (1–2) → pulled back over the kettle, on the far side from the worker (3). The robot doesn't just stop in place — it retracts to the side away from the predicted path, clearing the space the worker is about to occupy, and resumes once the worker leaves. Trigger = a predicted path entering the stop ring within the 1.6 s horizon (ISO/TS 15066 SSM).
