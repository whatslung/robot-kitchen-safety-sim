The recall–precision trade of learned prediction

Adopting the learned predictor (before = constant-velocity, after = LSTM) — danger-zone entry.

Before · constant-velocity After · learned (LSTM)

A deliberate, safety-positive trade. The learned predictor lifts danger-zone recall 57%→93% — it catches the curved approaches constant-velocity misses — at the cost of precision 96%→67%, i.e. more cautious false slowdowns. In safety, a missed entry is a possible collision while a false alarm only costs throughput, so trading precision for recall is the correct direction. 4-camera config, robot at origin (§5-15).
